## Background
<!-- type: background -->
Thiopurine S-methyltransferase (TPMT) inactivates the thiopurines azathioprine, mercaptopurine and thioguanine. Reduced TPMT activity causes accumulation of cytotoxic thioguanine nucleotides, which raises the risk of severe, potentially life-threatening myelosuppression at standard doses. Patients with two no-function TPMT alleles have little or no TPMT activity.

## Genotype to phenotype translation
<!-- type: translation -->
TPMT diplotype to phenotype translation:
- *1/*1: Normal Metabolizer.
- *1/*2, *1/*3A, *1/*3B, *1/*3C and *1/*4: Intermediate Metabolizer (one normal-function and one no-function allele).
- *2/*3A, *3A/*3A, *3A/*3C, *3C/*3C and *3C/*4: Poor Metabolizer (two no-function alleles).

## Recommendation: Normal Metabolizer (azathioprine)
<!-- type: recommendation; phenotype: Normal Metabolizer; drug: azathioprine; action: standard -->
For TPMT normal metabolizers, start azathioprine at the normal starting dose and adjust the dose according to disease-specific guidelines.

## Recommendation: Intermediate Metabolizer (azathioprine)
<!-- type: recommendation; phenotype: Intermediate Metabolizer; drug: azathioprine; action: reduce_dose -->
For TPMT intermediate metabolizers, start azathioprine at a reduced dose, about 30 to 80 percent of the normal starting dose, and adjust the dose based on the degree of myelosuppression and disease-specific guidelines. Allow 2 to 4 weeks to reach steady state after each dose adjustment.

## Recommendation: Poor Metabolizer (azathioprine)
<!-- type: recommendation; phenotype: Poor Metabolizer; drug: azathioprine; action: alternative_agent -->
For TPMT poor metabolizers, consider an alternative non-thiopurine immunosuppressant. If azathioprine is used, start at a drastically reduced dose, reducing the daily dose about 10-fold and dosing three times per week instead of daily, and adjust based on the degree of myelosuppression.

## Recommendation: Normal Metabolizer (mercaptopurine)
<!-- type: recommendation; phenotype: Normal Metabolizer; drug: mercaptopurine; action: standard -->
For TPMT normal metabolizers, start mercaptopurine at the normal starting dose and adjust the dose according to disease-specific guidelines.

## Recommendation: Intermediate Metabolizer (mercaptopurine)
<!-- type: recommendation; phenotype: Intermediate Metabolizer; drug: mercaptopurine; action: reduce_dose -->
For TPMT intermediate metabolizers, start mercaptopurine at a reduced dose, about 30 to 80 percent of the normal starting dose, and adjust the dose based on the degree of myelosuppression and disease-specific guidelines. Allow 2 to 4 weeks to reach steady state after each dose adjustment.

## Recommendation: Poor Metabolizer (mercaptopurine)
<!-- type: recommendation; phenotype: Poor Metabolizer; drug: mercaptopurine; action: alternative_agent -->
For TPMT poor metabolizers with a malignancy, start mercaptopurine at a drastically reduced dose, reducing the daily dose about 10-fold and dosing three times per week instead of daily. For non-malignant conditions, consider an alternative non-thiopurine agent.

## Caveats
<!-- type: caveat -->
CPIC thiopurine recommendations consider TPMT and NUDT15 together. This summary covers TPMT only, so NUDT15 status must be assessed separately before finalizing a dose. Complete blood counts should be monitored regardless of genotype.
