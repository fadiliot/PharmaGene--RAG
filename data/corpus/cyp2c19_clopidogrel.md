## Background
<!-- type: background -->
Clopidogrel is a prodrug that is bioactivated mainly by CYP2C19 to an active thiol metabolite, which inhibits the platelet P2Y12 receptor. CYP2C19 loss-of-function alleles (*2 and *3) reduce formation of the active metabolite and reduce platelet inhibition. In patients with acute coronary syndrome who undergo percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI), reduced CYP2C19 function is associated with a higher risk of major adverse cardiovascular events, including stent thrombosis and myocardial infarction. The *17 allele is an increased-function allele.

## Genotype to phenotype translation
<!-- type: translation -->
CYP2C19 diplotype to phenotype translation:
- *1/*1: Normal Metabolizer.
- *1/*17: Rapid Metabolizer.
- *17/*17: Ultrarapid Metabolizer.
- *1/*2 and *1/*3: Intermediate Metabolizer.
- *2/*17 and *3/*17: Intermediate Metabolizer.
- *2/*2, *2/*3 and *3/*3: Poor Metabolizer.

## Recommendation: Ultrarapid Metabolizer
<!-- type: recommendation; phenotype: Ultrarapid Metabolizer; drug: clopidogrel; action: standard -->
For CYP2C19 ultrarapid metabolizers with acute coronary syndrome or undergoing PCI, clopidogrel can be used at the standard dose of 75 mg per day.

## Recommendation: Rapid Metabolizer
<!-- type: recommendation; phenotype: Rapid Metabolizer; drug: clopidogrel; action: standard -->
For CYP2C19 rapid metabolizers with acute coronary syndrome or undergoing PCI, clopidogrel can be used at the standard dose of 75 mg per day.

## Recommendation: Normal Metabolizer
<!-- type: recommendation; phenotype: Normal Metabolizer; drug: clopidogrel; action: standard -->
For CYP2C19 normal metabolizers with acute coronary syndrome or undergoing PCI, clopidogrel can be used at the standard dose of 75 mg per day.

## Recommendation: Intermediate Metabolizer
<!-- type: recommendation; phenotype: Intermediate Metabolizer; drug: clopidogrel; action: alternative_agent -->
For CYP2C19 intermediate metabolizers with acute coronary syndrome or undergoing PCI, avoid standard-dose clopidogrel if possible. Use an alternative antiplatelet agent such as prasugrel or ticagrelor at the standard dose if there is no contraindication.

## Recommendation: Poor Metabolizer
<!-- type: recommendation; phenotype: Poor Metabolizer; drug: clopidogrel; action: alternative_agent -->
For CYP2C19 poor metabolizers with acute coronary syndrome or undergoing PCI, avoid standard-dose clopidogrel if possible. Use an alternative antiplatelet agent such as prasugrel or ticagrelor at the standard dose if there is no contraindication.

## Caveats
<!-- type: caveat -->
These recommendations apply to cardiovascular indications such as acute coronary syndrome and PCI. Recommendations for neurovascular indications differ and are not covered here. Prasugrel is contraindicated in patients with a history of stroke or transient ischemic attack, and both prasugrel and ticagrelor increase bleeding risk, so contraindications must be checked before switching. Drug interactions and adherence also influence clopidogrel response.
