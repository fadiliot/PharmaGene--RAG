## Background
<!-- type: background -->
Codeine is a prodrug that CYP2D6 converts to morphine, which is responsible for most of its analgesic effect. CYP2D6 ultrarapid metabolizers form morphine more rapidly and extensively, which increases the risk of morphine toxicity, including life-threatening respiratory depression. CYP2D6 poor metabolizers form little morphine and may get little or no pain relief.

## Genotype to phenotype translation
<!-- type: translation -->
CYP2D6 phenotype is assigned from an activity score, which is the sum of the activity values of the two alleles, with the value of a duplicated allele multiplied by its copy number. *1 and *2 have a value of 1. *9 and *17 have a value of 0.5. *10 and *41 have a value of 0.25. *3, *4, *5 and *6 have a value of 0.
- Activity score 0: Poor Metabolizer.
- Activity score 0.25 to 1.0: Intermediate Metabolizer.
- Activity score 1.25 to 2.25: Normal Metabolizer.
- Activity score greater than 2.25: Ultrarapid Metabolizer.
Examples: *1/*1 has an activity score of 2.0 (Normal Metabolizer). *1xN/*1 carries a gene duplication, so its activity score is at least 3.0 (Ultrarapid Metabolizer). *4/*4 has an activity score of 0 (Poor Metabolizer).

## Recommendation: Ultrarapid Metabolizer
<!-- type: recommendation; phenotype: Ultrarapid Metabolizer; drug: codeine; action: avoid -->
For CYP2D6 ultrarapid metabolizers, avoid codeine because of the potential for serious toxicity. If opioid use is warranted, consider a non-tramadol and non-codeine opioid.

## Recommendation: Normal Metabolizer
<!-- type: recommendation; phenotype: Normal Metabolizer; drug: codeine; action: standard -->
For CYP2D6 normal metabolizers, use the label-recommended age- or weight-specific dosing of codeine.

## Recommendation: Intermediate Metabolizer
<!-- type: recommendation; phenotype: Intermediate Metabolizer; drug: codeine; action: standard_monitor -->
For CYP2D6 intermediate metabolizers, use the label-recommended age- or weight-specific dosing of codeine. If there is no response and opioid use is warranted, consider a non-tramadol and non-codeine opioid.

## Recommendation: Poor Metabolizer
<!-- type: recommendation; phenotype: Poor Metabolizer; drug: codeine; action: avoid -->
For CYP2D6 poor metabolizers, avoid codeine because of the lack of efficacy. If opioid use is warranted, consider a non-tramadol and non-codeine opioid.

## Caveats
<!-- type: caveat -->
These recommendations apply to codeine for pain. Tramadol is also activated by CYP2D6 but is not covered in this summary. Gene duplication with an unspecified copy number (written xN) can make the phenotype uncertain unless the exact copy number is known.
