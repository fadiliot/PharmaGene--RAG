## Background
<!-- type: background -->
SLCO1B1 encodes the hepatic uptake transporter OATP1B1. Reduced-function variants, notably c.521T>C (which defines the *5 allele; the *15 allele carries c.521T>C together with c.388A>G), reduce hepatic uptake of simvastatin acid and increase its systemic exposure. Higher exposure increases the risk of statin-associated musculoskeletal symptoms, including myopathy and, rarely, rhabdomyolysis, especially at higher simvastatin doses.

## Genotype to phenotype translation
<!-- type: translation -->
SLCO1B1 diplotype to phenotype translation:
- *1/*1: Normal Function.
- *1/*5 and *1/*15: Decreased Function (one copy of the c.521T>C variant, heterozygous).
- *5/*5, *5/*15 and *15/*15: Poor Function (two copies of reduced-function alleles, homozygous).
The c.521T>C variant defines the *5 allele. Zygosity must be known to assign the phenotype.

## Recommendation: Normal Function
<!-- type: recommendation; phenotype: Normal Function; drug: simvastatin; action: standard -->
For SLCO1B1 normal function, prescribe the desired simvastatin starting dose and adjust the dose according to disease-specific guidelines.

## Recommendation: Decreased Function
<!-- type: recommendation; phenotype: Decreased Function; drug: simvastatin; action: reduce_dose -->
For SLCO1B1 decreased function there is an increased risk of myopathy with higher simvastatin doses. Prescribe a lower simvastatin dose or consider an alternative statin such as pravastatin or rosuvastatin, and consider routine creatine kinase surveillance.

## Recommendation: Poor Function
<!-- type: recommendation; phenotype: Poor Function; drug: simvastatin; action: alternative_agent -->
For SLCO1B1 poor function there is a high risk of myopathy with simvastatin. Prefer an alternative statin such as pravastatin or rosuvastatin. If simvastatin is still chosen, use a lower dose and consider routine creatine kinase surveillance.

## Caveats
<!-- type: caveat -->
One copy of the c.521T>C variant (heterozygous) corresponds to decreased function and two copies (homozygous) correspond to poor function, so a report that only states that the variant is present is not sufficient to choose a recommendation. Age, female sex, renal impairment, hypothyroidism and interacting drugs also raise the risk of statin myopathy.
